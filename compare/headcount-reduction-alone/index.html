agentworth vs Headcount reduction alone

How agentworth differs from headcount reduction alone as a way to measure AI agent ROI.

What headcount reduction alone actually is

Reporting the number of support or operations roles not backfilled, or roles eliminated outright, after an AI agent went live, and treating that as the dollar value the agent produced.

Where it breaks down

  • Headcount decisions usually have several causes at once, attrition, a budget freeze, a reorganization, and crediting the full reduction to the agent overstates what it actually did
  • It says nothing about whether the remaining team is now quietly absorbing more rework or more escalations because of gaps the agent left behind
  • It is a single point in time measurement that never updates, while an agent's actual performance can improve or decline every month after that headcount decision was made

How agentworth differs

agentworth ties savings to verified resolutions and their ongoing outcomes rather than a staffing count taken once, so the reported number keeps reflecting what the agent is doing right now instead of a decision made a year earlier for a mix of reasons.
