agentworth vs Vendor case studies from other customers

How agentworth differs from vendor case studies from other customers as a way to measure AI agent ROI.

What vendor case studies from other customers actually is

A published case study or benchmark figure from a different company already running the same AI agent platform, used as a stand in for what your own program is likely to achieve.

Where it breaks down

  • A case study customer's ticket mix, team structure, and starting cost per resolution are rarely close enough to yours for their percentage improvement to transfer directly to your own numbers
  • Vendors publish their best results, so any single case study is a sample of one taken from the high end of the outcome range, not a typical outcome
  • A published figure almost never shows enough of its own methodology to tell whether it was calculated the way you would calculate yours, so comparing the two numbers is comparing two different definitions wearing the same label

How agentworth differs

agentworth calculates ROI from your own resolutions and your own cost baseline instead of borrowing someone else's outcome, so the number you report is specific to what your agent actually did, not to what a different company's agent did somewhere else.
