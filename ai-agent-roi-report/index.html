What this actually means

Put these seven numbers side by side and one pattern shows up clearly. Spending on AI agents is accelerating fast, trillions of dollars fast, while the ability to prove any of it is working has not kept up at all. Most companies are not measuring badly, they are mostly not measuring at all, and the handful of CFOs who do trust an AI generated number can be counted in the teens, not the majority.
