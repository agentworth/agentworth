Containment rate

A plain definition of containment rate, and why it matters for AI agent ROI.

Definition

The share of conversations an AI agent resolves without any human involvement at all.

Why it matters

A high containment rate looks good on a slide, but it says nothing about whether those contained conversations were actually resolved well, which is why it should never be reported alone.
