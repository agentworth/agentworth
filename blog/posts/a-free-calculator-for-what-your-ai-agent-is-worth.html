A free calculator for what your AI agent is worth

We built agentworth to answer one question honestly: is your AI agent actually paying off. Before you connect anything or talk to us at all, you should be able to get a rough answer in under a minute. So we built a calculator for that, and it is free and public, no signup required.

You put in three numbers. How many tickets or requests your agent handles a month. What share of those it resolves without a human touching them. What it would have cost a human to do that instead. The calculator gives you an estimate of cost avoided this month, net value after what you pay for the agent, and a rough return multiple.

Why we are giving this away

Because most teams never sit down and do this math at all. They know the agent is busy. They do not have a number. A rough estimate, even a slightly wrong one, is still more than most people walk into a board meeting with. If this calculator is the most useful thing you get from us today, good, that was the point.

Where it falls short, on purpose

The calculator only knows what you type in. It cannot tell you if your real resolution rate matches what you guessed, or flag the handful of resolutions that quietly went wrong. That is the actual hard problem, and it is the one agentworth is built to solve. We connect to your agent platform directly, track every real resolution as it happens, and hand you a number with an audit trail behind it instead of an input box.

Try the calculator here: agentworth.cloud/roi-calculator. If the number surprises you, or you want to see what the verified version looks like, get in touch.
